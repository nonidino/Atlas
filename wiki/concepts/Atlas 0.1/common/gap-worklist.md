**Three findings that change work below rather than closing it.**

1. **$\tau$ is not agent infidelity.** It dominates at $99.8\%$ while the agent *is* the reference solver. It is $\Delta t$-independent across a $25\times$ range, lives away from the seams ($42\%$ beyond 32 cells), and is exactly the per-window pressure projection: `tile.last_mismatch` $=$ `tile.last_div` $=2.76\times10^{-4}$ against the monolith's $8\times10^{-15}$. **[[plug-in-composition-theorems]] §1.4's attribution theorem, observed** — a $\sigma$ one level down wearing a $\tau$ label. **W34 (depth-tag every defect) is promoted from tiny to necessary.**
2. **The interface condition is a steady condition, and the scheme built on a healthy operator is wrong.** The true trace does **not** satisfy $\sum_i\Lambda_i\lambda=\chi$ ($\lVert r\rVert$ goes $2.6421\times10^{-5}\to2.6520\times10^{-5}$ when it is substituted), so solving it exactly overshoots by $41\times$ — growing to $150\times$ as $\Delta t$ is refined — and makes one composed step $8.9\times$ worse than not solving it. **W7 (R9 on the time-integrated flux) and W17 (waveform relaxation) are both promoted**: see below.
3. **The master bound holds with every constant measured** — tight at $N=1$ (ratio $0.93$), $14\times$ loose asymptotically, and the measured error **saturates** at $5.4\times10^{-4}$ rather than drifting. First bound this vault has quoted rather than named.

## What this tier says about $\mathcal R(t)$

[[port-algebra-atlas-0.1]] §6 asks for the global power residual *every macro-step, in every case study, from now on*, and calls it *"the cheapest possible physical-plausibility monitor"*. Measured on CS-9, **it is blind to the coupling under test by six orders of magnitude**: $P_\Omega/P_\Gamma = -1.04\times10^{-6}$ and $E_{\text{el}}/E_{\text{th}} = 1.17\times10^{-6}$. Declaring the volumetric term does move the relative residual — $2.909\times10^{-6}$ to $1.871\times10^{-6}$, a real $36\%$ — but both numbers are the same order as the backward-Euler differencing error. **The right residual for a co-located split is the receiving subsystem's own balance**, which the volumetric term closes to first order in $\Delta t$ ($6.02\times10^{-2}$ down to $3.66\times10^{-3}$ over a $16\times$ range, $\log_2$ ratios $1.020$–$1.003$). That bounds where §6 applies; nothing here retracts it, and §10.3 now records the bound on the page itself.

## And the route that is worse than the wrong one

Two things the *closed* vocabulary can already express, measured side by side on identical physics:

- **`surface-mech`** — the classical equivalent thermal pressure $\mathbf t=\beta\,\Delta T\,\mathbf n$, a real `MECH` bond on a real surface. The exact identity $G=G_{\text{surf}}+G_{\text{body}}$ (to $10^{-15}$) says what it carries and what it drops; the dropped body force is only $9.58\%$ of the load, and because the two halves nearly cancel on a soft strip the displacement is amplified $233\times$ and the **stress is wrong by $1279\times$**.
- **`global-field`** — the eigenstrain declared a `GlobalField`, which bypasses L3 entirely. **Numerically exact**, and no scale set, prolongation, adjoint, null space, `response_half`, $\tau$, $\sigma$ or $\beta$ is ever asked for. Compiled against the `surface-mech` graph it turns $\mathrm{E3}$ from `fails` to `holds` and $\mathrm{E7}$ from `fails` to `unchecked`, and carries three fewer decertifications: **`L1/E3`, `L4/E7/passivity`, `L4/block-share` and `L4/operator-content` — four seam properties — are traded for `L3/graph`, one line saying it is not a composition.** It is not uniformly better and that is on the record too: $\beta$ is a property of a probed seam, so it becomes *unmeasurable* and the unmeasured list gets one longer.

> **Declaring a coupling *out* of the port algebra improves its envelope stamp**, because the quantities that would have failed are the ones a seam defines. It is declarable today by anyone, nothing in a compile says so, and it is the purest instance of the silent-wrongness class this project has produced. [[port-algebra-atlas-0.1]] §10.4 now states it; no rule catches it.

**The candidate discriminator is provenance, and on this census it separates cleanly.** Gravity is external and is what §5.1 wrote the class for. The four `pressure` declarations are a genuine *global operation over every agent at once*, owned by the composition layer and declared as such — the thing a domain decomposition legitimately cannot localize. The eigenstrain is neither: it is **one named agent's state, handed into another agent's update**, which is a seam with the seam removed. Only the last is CS-9's silent-wrongness route.

**So the rule is one line and the decision is not.** A rule that fires when a global field's value is produced by a *named proper subset* of the graph's agents catches `thermal-eigenstrain` and clears the other six. But the discriminator is **not declarable today** — that is the row — and the four `pressure` rows are the reason the fix is a judgement: `wake_array`'s pressure field is doing repair work on a checkpoint whose pressure channel is *"a PLACEHOLDER its loader pins to 0"*, which is closer to CS-9's route than to gravity, and it sits under a `partition_of_unity` and a `projected_assembly` that a global-solve reading assumes. Whether that one is external, global, or a fourth thing decides whether the rule has one exception or a class of them.

> **W117a — the judgement the rule could not make for itself. Run 2026-09-02.** Re-declare all seven with a provenance field, run the candidate rule, and compare the firing pattern against each declaration's own stated intent. Firing on exactly `thermal-eigenstrain` means the rule is right and the row closes. **Firing on the four `pressure` declarations too would mean either the rule is wrong or those four are the same defect as CS-9's route — and this vault's four largest case studies would then be resting on it.** The experiment was designed to be able to see that.
