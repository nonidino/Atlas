Wind-Farm Agent Graph

Eight agents forming a disjoint cover of the hub-height plane, joined by fifteen typed ports. Two experts: one frozen incompressible-NS network used five times, one zero-parameter actuator disk used twice. Nothing here is trained.

Reading the drawing

  • Upstream of rotor 1 the full height is one agent — no wake yet to separate from a bypass.
  • N and W are rectangles with a notch where the rotor strip sits.
  • The two open ROT ports carry the extracted shaft power out of the model.

What the topology says

  • The graph has cycles. I → N → F → W and I → B± → W are two distinct paths between the same agents, so message passing is path-dependent — the rocket's near-chain graph never tested this.
  • The bypass is load-bearing. Remove B± and the wake agents have no momentum source; the deficit becomes permanent. It is the most visible possible failure mode.
  • The disks are the only heterogeneous nodes. Every solid line into a hatched box is a closed-form expert meeting a learned one — the coupling this case study exists to test.

Enforcement is structural, not editorial. The four bold edges are disk faces, where one side is closed-form and the flux residual is therefore enforceable by a minimum-norm projection. The other eleven have a learned expert on both sides and a frozen model on each — nothing to project onto, nothing to train against — so their residual is measured and reported, never claimed as conserved.
