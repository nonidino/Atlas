The five claims, on the record

The rocket episode: a correct multi-solver coupled model

Seven agents plus a trajectory, each a build-repo solver imported unmodified: reacting chamber and nozzle, plume, external air, a conducting and bending shell, and a rigid-body ODE. 29 macro-steps of 5 ms, launched at 25 km and Mach 3.5. This is a correctness record. No full-domain run exists to compare its speed or accuracy against.

outcome-c5-requirements-for-dd-native-experts §2

Not done

  1. C1The speedup is one family, one laptop, Python threads. No full-domain run of the rocket, and no equal-cost comparison, the only kind that could show decomposition winning on accuracy.
  2. C2No learned expert admitted with a meaningful contribution. The matched shrink (W214) diverges early; a residual-scheduled shrink (W347) and a second donor (W120) are untested.
  3. C3One family, one host class. At the coupled seams measured, the coarse classical competitor is correct but costs more than the cold march, so the bar there is the cold march.
  4. C4Learned experts only in 2-D incompressible flow. No march spans the vehicle's 400× clock range; 3-D is a classical half-car box.
  5. C5No architecture, no training data, no fine-tuning run, no new expert. Light adaptation was priced at 0.35 h per 2000 iterations and not run.
