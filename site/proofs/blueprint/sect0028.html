7.4 A remark beside T1: accuracy is not a substitute

In plain words.

T1 asks that the learned map contract. It cannot be replaced by “the classical map contracts and the learned map is uniformly close to it”. The example below satisfies both of those, and its learned iteration alternates between two points for ever.

Example 88 The SNI counterexample

Uses

  • Theorem 1

Lean declarations

Let \(T(u)=\tfrac 12u\) and \(\tilde T(u)=\tfrac 12\bigl(u-\tanh 10u\bigr)\) on \(\mathbb {R}\). Then \(T\) is a contraction with factor \(\tfrac 12\) and fixed point \(0\); \(|\tilde T(u)-T(u)|\le \tfrac 12\) for every \(u\); and there is an \(x\in [0.3,\, 0.34]\) with \(\tilde T(x)=-x\) and \(\tilde T(-x)=x\), so that \(\tilde T^k(x)=(-1)^kx\) for every \(k\).

Proof ▶

\(\tilde T(x)=-x\) means \(\tanh 10x=3x\). The function \(\tanh 10x-3x\) is continuous, positive at \(0.3\) (since \(e^6{\gt}19\) gives \(\tanh 3{\gt}0.9\)) and negative at \(0.34\) (since \(\tanh {\lt}1{\lt}1.02\)); the intermediate value theorem gives \(x\). \(\tilde T\) is odd.
