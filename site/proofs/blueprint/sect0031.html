8.2 T22: the master bound with learned experts

In plain words.

The master bound (T4) adds up one-step defects. For a coupled step it names three: \(\tau \), the experts being wrong even with the right interface data; \(\sigma \), the interface problem being posed with the wrong operator; \(\gamma \), the interface solve being stopped early. At tier 2 the learned experts put \(C_\mu \, \delta /(1-\rho )\) into \(\sigma \) and \(C_\mu \, \rho ^k\) times the initial interface error into \(\gamma \). At tier 0 there is no iteration, so there is no \(\gamma \), and the learned part of the defect is the Galerkin error that T25 bounds.

Let \(\Lambda \) be a complete metric space of interface data, and let the step \(\Phi (\lambda ,v)\) satisfy \(\lVert \Phi (\lambda ,v)-\Phi (\lambda ',v)\rVert \le C_\mu \, d(\lambda ,\lambda ')\) at the state \(v\). Let \(T_c(\lambda ^\star )=\lambda ^\star \), let \(T_l\) be a \(\rho \)-contraction with \(0\le \rho {\lt}1\), and \(d(T_l\lambda ^\star ,T_c\lambda ^\star )\le \delta \). Then \(T_l\) has a fixed point \(\lambda ^\dagger \), and for every \(\lambda ^{(0)}\) and \(k\),

\[ \lVert \Phi (\lambda ^\dagger ,v)-\Phi (\lambda ^\star ,v)\rVert \le \frac{C_\mu \, \delta }{1-\rho }, \qquad \lVert \Phi (T_l^{\, k}\lambda ^{(0)},v)-\Phi (\lambda ^\dagger ,v)\rVert \le C_\mu \, \rho ^k\, d(\lambda ^{(0)},\lambda ^\dagger ). \]
Proof ▶

Theorem 1 gives \(\lambda ^\dagger \), its distance to \(\lambda ^\star \) and the rate; then the Lipschitz bound of the step.

Let the step use the datum returned by \(k\) learned sweeps, \(u^{n+1}=\Phi \bigl(T_l(u^n)^k\lambda ^{(0)}(u^n),\, u^n\bigr)\), and magnify the difference between the computed and the true trajectory by at most \(L\ge 0\). Along the true trajectory \(u^{n,\star }\) let the hypotheses of Lemma 94 hold with constants \(C_\mu ,\rho ,\delta \), and let the starting guess be within \(D\) of the learned fixed point. Then

\[ \lVert u^N-u^{N,\star }\rVert \le L^N\lVert u^0-u^{0,\star }\rVert +\sum _{n=1}^{N}L^{N-n}\Bigl(\lVert \tau ^n\rVert +\frac{C_\mu \, \delta }{1-\rho }+C_\mu \, \rho ^k\, D\Bigr), \]

with \(\tau ^n=\Phi (\lambda ^{n-1,\star },u^{n-1,\star })-u^{n,\star }\).

Proof ▶

In the energy size \(\lVert \cdot \rVert _m\) of a quadratic energy: let \(u^{n,\mathrm{ex}}\) be the undivided discrete step from the true state \(u^{n,\star }\), and \(\tilde u^n\) the tier-0 step from it. If \(\lVert u^{n+1}-\tilde u^n\rVert _m\le L\lVert u^n-u^{n,\star }\rVert _m\), \(\lVert u^{n,\mathrm{ex}}-u^{n+1,\star }\rVert _m\le \tau ^n\) and \(\lVert u^{n,\mathrm{ex}}-\tilde u^n\rVert _m\le g^n\), then

\[ \lVert u^N-u^{N,\star }\rVert _m\le L^N\lVert u^0-u^{0,\star }\rVert _m +\sum _{n=0}^{N-1}L^{N-1-n}\bigl(\tau ^n+g^n\bigr). \]

There is no incomplete-solve term. For the tier-0 solve of Definition 68, under assumptions 1 and 2, \(g^n\) may be taken as the bound of Theorem 72: the error of any reference field plus the energy of the network’s field errors.

Proof ▶

\(u^{n+1}-u^{n+1,\star }=(u^{n+1}-\tilde u^n)+(\tilde u^n-u^{n,\mathrm{ex}}) +(u^{n,\mathrm{ex}}-u^{n+1,\star })\), the triangle inequality of the energy size, and the discrete Gronwall inequality.
