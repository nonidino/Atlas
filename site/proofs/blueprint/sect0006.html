2.1 T4: recursion, split, accumulation, regimes

In plain words.

  • Recursion. The new error is the old error carried through one step, plus the one-step defect: what a single step does wrong when it starts from the true solution.

  • Three-term split. With a coupling, the defect is a sum of three parts: the experts being wrong even when handed the true interface data (\(\tau \)), the interface problem being posed with the wrong operator (\(\sigma \)), and the interface solve being stopped early (\(\gamma \)). Iterating the coupling reduces \(\gamma \) and nothing else.

  • Accumulation. If one step magnifies a difference by at most \(L\), the error after \(N\) steps is at most \(L^N\) times the initial error, plus every defect magnified by the steps that follow it.

  • Three regimes. With every defect at most \(\delta \): the error stays below \(\delta /(1-L)\) for all time when \(L{\lt}1\); it grows at most linearly, \(N\delta \), when \(L=1\); the bound grows exponentially when \(L{\gt}1\).

Lemma 9 T4a, the exact error recursion

  • Atlas.error_recursion

Let \(E\) be a normed space, \(\Phi :E\to E\), and \(u,u^\star :\mathbb {N}\to E\) with \(u^{n+1}=\Phi (u^n)\). Then for every \(n\)

\[ u^{n+1}-u^{n+1,\star } =\bigl[\Phi (u^n)-\Phi (u^{n,\star })\bigr] +\bigl[\Phi (u^{n,\star })-u^{n+1,\star }\bigr]. \]

The second bracket is the one-step defect \(d^{n+1}\).

Proof ▶

Add and subtract \(\Phi (u^{n,\star })\).

Lemma 10 T4b, the three-term split

  • Atlas.defect_split

Let \(\Phi _\mu :E\to E\) be the composed step forced to use the interface datum \(\mu \), and \(\mathcal S:E\to E\) the exact evolution. For any three data \(\lambda ^\star \), \(\lambda ^\dagger \), \(\lambda ^{(k)}\) and any state \(v\),

\[ \Phi _{\lambda ^{(k)}}(v)-\mathcal S(v) =\underbrace{\Phi _{\lambda ^\star }(v)-\mathcal S(v)}_{\tau } +\underbrace{\Phi _{\lambda ^\dagger }(v)-\Phi _{\lambda ^\star }(v)}_{\sigma } +\underbrace{\Phi _{\lambda ^{(k)}}(v)-\Phi _{\lambda ^\dagger }(v)}_{\gamma }. \]
Proof ▶

The sum telescopes.

Lemma 11 T4c, accumulation
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • Atlas.accumulation

Let \(L\ge 0\) and \(e,d:\mathbb {N}\to \mathbb {R}\) with \(e_{n+1}\le L\, e_n+d_{n+1}\) for all \(n\). Then for every \(N\)

\[ e_N\le L^N e_0+\sum _{n=1}^{N}L^{N-n}d_n . \]
Proof ▶

Induction on \(N\). The step multiplies the bound for \(N\) by \(L\ge 0\) and adds \(d_{N+1}\).

  • Atlas.master_bound

Let \(u^{n+1}=\Phi (u^n)\), and suppose \(\lVert \Phi (u^n)-\Phi (u^{n,\star })\rVert \le L\, \lVert u^n-u^{n,\star }\rVert \) for every \(n\), with \(L\ge 0\). Then for every \(N\)

\[ \lVert u^N-u^{N,\star }\rVert \le L^N\lVert u^0-u^{0,\star }\rVert +\sum _{n=1}^{N}L^{N-n}\, \lVert \Phi (u^{n-1,\star })-u^{n,\star }\rVert . \]

The stability hypothesis is needed only along the two trajectories.

Proof ▶

Take norms in Lemma 9 and apply Lemma 11 to \(e_n=\lVert u^n-u^{n,\star }\rVert \).

Let the composed step be \(\Phi (v)=\Phi _{\lambda ^{(k)}(v)}(v)\), where \(\lambda ^{(k)}(v)\) is the datum the interface solver returns at the state \(v\). Under the hypotheses of Theorem 12, for any sequences of data \(\lambda ^{\star }_n\), \(\lambda ^{\dagger }_n\),

\[ \lVert u^N-u^{N,\star }\rVert \le L^N\lVert u^0-u^{0,\star }\rVert +\sum _{n=1}^{N}L^{N-n}\bigl(\tau ^n+\sigma ^n+\gamma ^n\bigr), \]

with \(\tau ^n\), \(\sigma ^n\), \(\gamma ^n\) the norms of the three terms of Lemma 10 at \(v=u^{n-1,\star }\) and \(\mathcal S(u^{n-1,\star })=u^{n,\star }\).

Proof ▶

Lemma 10 and the triangle inequality bound each defect by \(\tau ^n+\sigma ^n+\gamma ^n\); the weights \(L^{N-n}\) are non-negative.

Proposition 17 The regimes are attained
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • Atlas.master_bound_attained

For every \(L,\delta \in \mathbb {R}\) and every \(N\) there are real sequences with \(u^{n+1}=L\, u^n\), a one-step defect equal to \(\delta \) at every step and no initial error, whose error after \(N\) steps is exactly \(\sum _{i{\lt}N}L^i\, \delta \).

Proof ▶

\(u^n=0\) and \(u^{n+1,\star }=L\, u^{n,\star }-\delta \) with \(u^{0,\star }=0\).
