6.3 T24: perturbation of the interface solve

In plain words.

The exact interface system is \(\mathsf Sc=\chi \) and the host solves a learned one, \(\tilde{\mathsf S}\tilde c=\tilde\chi \). The two answers differ by at most the error of the matrix times the size of the exact answer, plus the error of the load, all divided by the smallest stretch \(\beta \) of the learned matrix. For the Gram construction that constant need not be measured on the learned matrix: by T25 (iii) the exact problem’s own constant serves.

Let \(\mathsf S,\tilde{\mathsf S}\) be bounded linear maps between real normed spaces with \(\beta \lVert x\rVert \le \lVert \tilde{\mathsf S}x\rVert \) for all \(x\) and some \(\beta {\gt}0\). If \(\mathsf Sc=\chi \) and \(\tilde{\mathsf S}\tilde c=\tilde\chi \),

\[ \lVert \tilde c-c\rVert \le \frac{\lVert \mathsf S-\tilde{\mathsf S}\rVert \, \lVert c\rVert +\lVert \chi -\tilde\chi \rVert }{\beta }. \]
Proof ▶

\(\tilde{\mathsf S}(\tilde c-c)=(\tilde\chi -\chi )+(\mathsf S-\tilde{\mathsf S})c\); take norms and use the lower bound.

On a real inner-product space, if \(\beta \lVert x\rVert ^2\le \langle \mathsf Sx,x\rangle \le \langle \tilde{\mathsf S}x,x\rangle \) for all \(x\) and some \(\beta {\gt}0\), the bound of Proposition 74 holds with this \(\beta \).

Proof ▶

\(\beta \lVert x\rVert ^2\le \langle \tilde{\mathsf S}x,x\rangle \le \lVert \tilde{\mathsf S}x\rVert \, \lVert x\rVert \) by Cauchy–Schwarz.
