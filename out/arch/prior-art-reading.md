# Prior-art reading for the chart operator (architecture chat, second session)

Read 2026-09-30 in full from arXiv HTML (and the published HTML for DNO), in a cloud container
with open network. Fifteen papers named in the hand-off, plus seven more the reading turned up
(marked +). For each: inputs and outputs, how geometry enters, the transmission condition, the
convergence theory and its hypotheses, and the speed baseline. Quotes are the papers' own
numbers; nothing here is re-measured. The N1-N6 verdicts are at the end.

## 1. Learned local solvers composed by Schwarz iteration

| work | in -> out | geometry | transmission | theory | speed baseline |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SNI** -- Huang, Zhang, Wu, Cheng (Bosch), ICLR 2026, arXiv 2504.00510 | GNOT; (subdomain mesh, boundary values, coefficient fields) -> solution; stationary 2-D, heat by space-time | random simple polygons (3-12 vertices) in training; at inference METIS pieces of a triangulation, extended by d layers; translation/scaling normalisation by PDE symmetry | **Dirichlet**, overlapping additive Schwarz-Richardson, step tau < 1/K; no coarse space | Thm 1: classical map contracts (rho) + uniform local error c => iterates within c' = tau t c/(1-rho) of classical, "and SNI converges". **The convergence step does not follow** (proof bounds ||u~^m-u~^n|| by ||u^m-u^n|| + 2c', not Cauchy). Counterexample: scripts/arch_sni_counterexample.py (both hypotheses hold, iterates 2-cycle at +-0.3325). The distance bound holds | **none against a classical solver**; baseline is GNOT direct inference; errors 2-11 % l2 |
| **NEST** -- Secchi, Balint, Maurizi, arXiv 2605.12343 (May 2026) | GNO on the 4x4x4 node graph of a 3x3x3 voxel patch; Dirichlet data extended by inverse-distance-squared -> displacement; a second net -> one gradient component | voxel active-cell sets (face-connected) | **Dirichlet**, overlapping additive Schwarz, partition of unity; **no coarse space** | none | vs single-core FEniCS, on a GPU: 111x at 15^3 falling to **3.2x at 60^3** because **iterations grow 4 -> 61** (their own diagnosis: one-level Schwarz; "multilevel coarse corrections ... natural directions") |
| **L-DDM** -- Wu, Kovachki, Liu, arXiv 2507.17328 (CMAME 2026) | PPNO (U-shaped interpolated-convolution operator); (microstructure a, IDW-extended boundary data) -> u, and a second model -> grad u | **square subdomains congruent to the training square** (affine maps); conclusion names "appropriate geometric mappings" as future work | **Dirichlet**, overlapping additive Schwarz, partition of unity | existence: for alternating Schwarz, a neural operator whose n-fold composition is within eps of u on compact sets (C^s spaces) | **none**; ~100 iterations on a 10x10 grid of squares, ~20 on L- and I-shapes |
| **Mosaic Flows** -- Wang, Planas, Chandramowlishwaran, Bostanabad, CMAME 389 (2022), arXiv 2104.10873 | GFNet (PINN-trained) on a unit-square "genome": (boundary samples, x) -> u(x); Laplace, NS | square genomes, basic + auxiliary | **Dirichlet**, alternating Schwarz with auxiliary genomes (12x fewer iterations than without) | classical E < 1 cited; "if GFNet's error -> 0 the prediction converges" (no theorem for the learned case) | **against a PINN** (1-3 orders); a 1200x domain takes ~3 h on a V100. Sec. 4.4: **exact Dirichlet lifting u = G + phi N** (after Lagaris/Sukumar) |
| + **SNAP-DDM** -- Mao, Lupoiu, Dai, Chen, Fan, ICML 2024, arXiv 2405.02351 | SM-FNO (self-modulated FNO) on fixed 64x64 subdomains; (material/source/PML image, **Robin boundary values**) -> field | square subdomains; specialised models per subdomain kind | **Robin, overlapping Schwarz** (EM: jk E - dE/dn; fluid too) | none; "subdomain solvers require near-unity accuracy" | vs FDFD at a 15 % mean-absolute-error target |
| + **Mao & Fan**, "Accurate and scalable deep Maxwell solvers using multilevel iterative methods", arXiv 2509.03622 (2025) | FNO preconditioner: (residual, auxiliary maps) -> error; used inside **F-GMRES per subdomain** to a residual threshold | fixed-size subdomains, quad-tree | **Robin**, one- and **two-level** overlapping Schwarz; **coarse space from Robin-to-Robin maps probed with the learned solver**, momentum damping | accuracy bounded by the inner Krylov residual (the network changes speed, not the answer) | **~2x slower than PARDISO on 80 CPU cores** (their own statement); training recurrent inside the F-GMRES loop |
| + **DD-DeepONet**, arXiv 2508.02717 (2025) | DeepONets per simple piece | rectangles/cuboids, "stretching transformations" to simple shapes | overlapping Dirichlet, and **Lions' non-overlapping Robin** | none | seconds per solve; no classical comparison of iterations |
| + **Hybrid FE-neural operator, non-overlapping** -- arXiv 2606.08796 (2026) | Point-DeepONet on unstructured FE point clouds | non-convex NO subdomain | Neumann-Dirichlet (non-overlapping) | none | **FE-NO vs FE-FE like-for-like**: iterations 26 vs 25 (3-D), wall 1.75-5.76x faster |

| **DIMON** -- Yin, Charon, Brody, Lu, Trayanova, Maggioni, Nature Comput. Sci. ## 5. Training on derivatives, and the backbone

- **DINO** -- O'Leary-Roseberry, Chen, Villa, Ghattas, J. Comput. Phys. 496 (2024), arXiv 2206.10745: H^1_nu loss (values + Frobenius of the parametric Jacobian), made affordable by truncated SVD / reduced bases and matrix-free JVPs through adjoints. Aimed at outer-loop derivatives (optimisation, inverse problems), not at a DD rate.
- **CNO** -- Raonic et al., NeurIPS 2023, arXiv 2302.01178: an operator U-Net with sinc-filtered up/down-sampling, representation-equivalent on bandlimited spaces, universal. **Its filters act on the periodic extension of the signal** (Appendix A.4), so on a square with non-periodic sides it needs an extension choice exactly as FNO does. Version 0's "FNO assumes periodicity, CNO does not" is withdrawn.

## 6. What this changes in dd-neural-prior-art-2026 section 3

| # | claim | verdict | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| N1 | one expert contract across physics: typed effort-flow ports, a compiler admits or refuses each seam | **confirmed, not found anywhere**. Every learned-DD paper above is one PDE family with its own coupling code; SNAP-DDM's "specialised" models are one physics. The classical coupling ingredient (wave coordinates on port-Hamiltonian ports) is published and recent (Wei et al. 2026) and must be cited | sections 1, 4 |
| N2 | convergence certificate independent of training quality | **narrowed**. Two routes exist in print: (i) make the local solve exact by iterating a classical residual around the network (HINTS; Mao & Fan 2025: F-GMRES per subdomain) -- convergence whatever the training, at classical-residual cost; (ii) wave-domain splitting with a checkable firm-non-expansiveness condition for classical lumped subsystems (Wei et al. 2026). **Not found: a certified bound on the learned scattering map itself that gives geometric convergence at network speed, with no classical residual in the loop, and an a-priori distance delta/(1-rho).** SNI's theorem claims convergence from accuracy alone and that step fails (counterexample) | sections 1, 4; scripts/arch_sni_counterexample.py |
| N3 | training aimed at the iteration count | **narrowed**. Iteration-aware training is published for learned *interface conditions* (spectral-radius loss, Taghibakhshi et al. 2022) and learned *preconditioners* (Mao & Fan 2025, training inside the F-GMRES loop). **Not found: a learned local solver trained on its response (Jacobian) to interface data so that the outer DD iteration with learned solves keeps the classical count.** Every learned local solver above is trained on states (plus PDE/BC residuals in SNAP-DDM) | sections 1, 4, 5 |
| N4 | charts with the metric passed explicitly | **confirmed as a design choice, not the novelty**. DIMON passes a PCA shape code, DNO the coordinate field X(xi), Geo-FNO nothing explicit; L-DDM/SNI/DD-DeepONet use affine or stretching maps to simple pieces. None passes the pulled-back tensor J DF^-1 kappa DF^-T per chart of a decomposition | section 2 |
| N5 | a measured specification of failure (S1-S12) | **confirmed**: no paper reports what a DD-native expert must satisfy from measured failures. Honest caveat: one checkpoint (W120) | all |
| N6 | machine-checked convergence and error theorems | **confirmed as not found** in any paper read; its status in the proposal depends on the proofs chat's record | all |

**To add to "must not claim":** Robin/optimized transmission with learned subdomain solvers (SNAP-DDM 2024, DD-DeepONet 2025, Mao & Fan 2025); coarse spaces built from learned subdomain solvers (Mao & Fan 2025); a learned solver as a preconditioner whose answer is exact (HINTS, Mao & Fan); exact Dirichlet lifting u = G + phi N (Mosaic Flows 4.4) and IDW boundary extension (L-DDM, NEST); iteration-count-aware training in general (Taghibakhshi et al. 2022; Mao & Fan 2025); wave-variable coupling with checkable non-expansiveness (Wei et al. 2026, classical); the harmonic map as the reference map (DNO).

**A correction to the prior-art page's E3 note:** "no published work reports iterations against a like-for-like classical local solver" is too strong: the hybrid FE-NO paper (2606.08796) reports FE-NO against FE-FE iteration counts and wall time. What is still missing is that comparison **for a domain fully tiled by learned experts at the same transmission condition**, which is E3.

**Speed baselines, collected:** only Mao & Fan compare against an optimised direct solver, and they are about 2x slower (2-D, a few million unknowns). NEST's speed-up falls with resolution because one-level iteration counts grow. SNI and L-DDM report no timing. GINO's, Geo-FNO's and DIMON's large factors are single-shot whole-domain surrogates against full solvers, not decompositions.
